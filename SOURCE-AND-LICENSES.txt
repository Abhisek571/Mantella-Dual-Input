Mantella duel input v1.0

Unofficial modifications to Mantella, dated 2026-09-09: separate typed/spoken
reply controls and a shorter MCM layout. Not endorsed by the Mantella authors.

BUILDING
The package supplies the modified backend modules and archive helper in
Source/backend, with a build contract recording source and executable hashes.
Run setup_backend.py using CPython 3.11 against the supported original executable;
see INSTALL.txt. No third-party Python packages are needed for this patch step.
Papyrus sources require the Skyrim Papyrus compiler and Mantella's original
SKSE, SkyUI, UIExtensions and SKSE_HTTP script imports to rebuild.

No Mantella executable, bundled runtime, dependency scripts, or voice assets
are redistributed here. Obtain them from the original Mantella installation.
Setup creates a copy and does not alter the original or user configuration.
The hash guards establish release compatibility, not restrictions on license
rights: developers may change the source and build contract for their own builds.
Redistributing the resulting full executable requires reviewing its bundled
components and satisfying their source and notice requirements separately.
Retain applicable AGPL notices and source access when serving remote users.
